Frequently asked questions

Is the password generator free?

Yes. Ciphra builds passwords entirely in your browser and is completely free, with no sign-up.

Are my passwords uploaded?

Never. Generation happens locally with crypto.getRandomValues(); nothing leaves your device.

Can I exclude similar characters?

Yes. Tick "Exclude ambiguous" to remove characters like 0O1lI that are easy to mix up.
