Frequently asked questions

Does it store my password?

No. Analysis happens entirely in your browser; the password is never stored or transmitted.

What makes a password strong?

Length plus a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols, with no common words or repeated runs.

How is strength measured?

Ciphra estimates entropy in bits from length and character variety, then applies pattern penalties.
