Frequently asked questions

How does encryption work?

Text is encrypted with AES-GCM 256-bit; the key is derived from your passphrase using PBKDF2 (SHA-256, 150k iterations).

Where is my plaintext sent?

Nowhere. Encryption and decryption run entirely on your device.

Can I decrypt later?

Yes. Keep the ciphertext (salt.iv.ciphertext) and the same passphrase to decrypt anytime.
