Frequently asked questions

What is a passphrase?

A passphrase is several random words joined together — easier to remember than a random password yet still strong.

Is it random?

Yes. Words are drawn from a local list using a cryptographically secure random source.

Is it private?

Yes. Everything runs in your browser; no phrase is sent anywhere.
